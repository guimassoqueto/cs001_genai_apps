# Choose the Right AI Model for Your Use Case

## Important: this doc is for reference only and MUST NOT be used to generate a Python script

This module content explains how to effectively select, use, and maintain AI models for business applications, using the analogy of growing a garden with different vegetables.

Choosing and Using AI Models
* Employ a multi-model approach by using various AI models tailored to specific use cases, ensuring flexibility and better fit.
* Start with a clear, specific prompt that defines the use case, problem, and desired outcomes to guide model selection and testing.

Evaluation and Ongoing Management
* Research models based on size, performance, cost, risks, and deployment methods, then test them against your prompt to find the best fit.
* Continuously evaluate and govern models through ongoing testing, updating data and prompts, and experimenting with new models to maintain relevance and performance.

Considerations and Collaboration
* Factor in performance, accuracy, reliability, speed, size, transparency, deployment, and risks when choosing models.
* Implement AI solutions with cross-disciplinary teams to monitor performance benchmarks and ensure informed decision-making.

Continuous Care and Optimization
* Treat AI models like a garden that requires ongoing care, including testing, governance, and optimization to keep models effective.
* Adapt strategies as models evolve and business needs change to avoid stagnation and ensure sustained success.
